03 — ABOUT PAGE
About Mohammed Rizwan
A personal and professional biography focused on resilience, international experience and business leadership.
PAGE HERO HEADING
A Life Defined by Movement, Hard Work and Enterprise
HERO TEXT
From early experiences in Pakistan to employment in Qatar and entrepreneurship in the United States, Mohammed Rizwan has built his life by embracing opportunity, navigating change and staying closely connected to the work.
Full Biography
Mohammed Rizwan was born in Pakistan, where he completed his basic education and developed the practical mindset that would later define his professional life. During his early years, he took on a variety of small jobs and responsibilities. These experiences taught him the value of earning trust, adapting quickly and never considering honest work beneath him.
His journey later took him to Qatar, where he worked and gained valuable international exposure. Living and working away from home strengthened his independence and broadened his understanding of people, markets and opportunity. It was a demanding stage of life, but it prepared him for an even larger transition.
After moving to the United States, Mohammed Rizwan entered the automotive industry. Rather than viewing it as a single line of business, he learned the industry from the inside: vehicles, repairs, customer service, auctions, rental operations and sales. His first major entrepreneurial turning point came with Cars Compound, an auto body shop in Marietta. The business gave him a foundation, a deeper understanding of the market and the confidence to expand.
Over time, he acquired a Priceless Car Rental franchise and later established VIP Kars, a car dealership. His activities continued to grow across automotive services, rental vehicles, dealerships, junkyard operations and tourism. Each venture added a new layer of knowledge, but his working style remained the same: stay involved, understand the customer and solve problems directly.
That hands-on approach eventually inspired his most ambitious project, YalaRide. By combining decades of real-world experience with technology, Mohammed Rizwan set out to create a global car-rental marketplace that could make vehicle access easier, more transparent and more affordable for customers while creating new opportunities for rental businesses.
Today, his earlier ventures continue to operate while he leads the growth of YalaRide. His story is not defined by a single achievement. It is a continuing journey of building, learning and creating businesses that serve people in practical ways.
Personal Philosophy
“I have always believed that no work is too small and no challenge is too great when you are committed to your vision.”
— Mohammed Rizwan

Why YalaRide Was Created
After years of working directly in the automotive and rental industries, Mohammed Rizwan understood the same challenges from both sides. Customers wanted more accessible choices and better value. Rental businesses needed stronger visibility and new ways to connect with renters. YalaRide was created to bring those needs together through one modern marketplace.
Built from Real Industry Experience
YalaRide is not an idea developed from a distance. Its direction comes from decades spent managing vehicles, attending auctions, operating rental businesses, responding to customer concerns and understanding the practical details that shape the rental experience. Approximately eighteen months of focused development transformed that knowledge into a technology platform with global potential.
